1. Identity and discovery

These fields create the card and make the recipe searchable. Existing values are suggested, but new categories and tags are allowed.

2. Ingredients

Use one complete ingredient per field. Include quantity, unit, preparation and whether it is optional.

3. Method

Give each step a short stage and one complete action. Include texture, visual or doneness cues when they change the outcome.

4. The details that give it soul

Add only notes that prevent failure, explain why something works, or improve finishing, serving, storage or substitutions.
